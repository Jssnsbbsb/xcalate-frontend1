6 modes

Walk the centre

Khwairamband Bazaar, Ima Keithel, Thangal Bazaar, and Kangla work well as a slow walking loop.

Best for: markets and heritage lanes

Bus

Use local buses for budget trips across Imphal and nearby towns. Ask the conductor for the route and carry smaller notes.

Tip: confirm the last bus back

Auto-rickshaw

Autos are practical for short city rides. Try the stands around Polo Ground and Nagamapal, and agree the fare first.

Best for: quick neighbourhood hops

Bike

Rent only through a current, verified local contact. Check your licence, helmet, papers, fuel, and the road conditions.

Bring: ID, licence, and a rain layer

Car with driver

For Loktak, Andro, Ukhrul, or a full-day route, a local driver is usually easier than self-driving unfamiliar roads.

Ask for: rate, fuel, and waiting time

Cab

Ask your stay to arrange a verified cab for airport transfers and longer rides. Confirm the current fare before booking.

Reliable first step: ask your homestay

Getting around the city.

Imphal is easiest when you combine short walks with local rides. Choose the pace and transport that fit your day.

Slow city, close landmarks

Walk the centre

Walking works well around Khwairamband Bazaar, Ima Keithel, Thangal Bazaar, Kangla, and nearby market streets. Go slowly and take care around busy crossings.

Best forMarkets, heritage lanes, and short city hops
Easy walking loopKangla → Ima Keithel → Thangal Bazaar → Khwairamband Bazaar
Budget travel across the city

Buses and city stops

Use local buses for budget trips across Imphal and between nearby towns. Ask the conductor for the route, confirm the destination before boarding, and keep smaller notes ready.

Khwairamband BazaarCentral market area; ask for routes toward Ima Keithel and the city centre.
BT Road / Paona BazaarBusy central corridor for market and commercial-area connections.
Thangal BazaarCentral shopping district; check with conductors for onward routes.
NagamapalImportant local transport area; ask for the final stop before boarding.
Polo GroundUseful reference point for city connections and nearby auto stands.
Airport Road / Imphal AirportConfirm the last city connection and keep a cab backup for late arrivals.
Bus-stop tipAsk “which bus goes toward...” and show the place name on your phone.
Quick neighbourhood connections

Auto-rickshaws

Autos are useful for short journeys. Signal one from the roadside or try stands behind Polo Ground and around Nagamapal.

Before you rideAgree the fare and destination first
